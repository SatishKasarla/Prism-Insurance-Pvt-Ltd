# Prism-Insurance-Pvt-Ltd 

1. Project Title / Headline

A dynamic, interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Prism Insurance Pvt Ltd’s insurance business data, focusing on policy performance, premium collection, claim analysis, customer demographics, policy distribution, and key business performance indicators to support strategic decision-making.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The Prism Insurance Pvt Ltd Dashboard is a visually engaging and analytical Power BI report designed to help organizations monitor and analyze insurance business data. The dashboard focuses on key metrics such as total policies, premium amount, claim amount, claim status, policy types, customer demographics, and business performance trends.

This dashboard is intended for Insurance Managers, Business Analysts, Insurance Operations Teams, Management Executives, and Decision Makers who seek to understand insurance performance, monitor claims, analyze customer behavior, and make data-driven business decisions.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.

📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer used for preparing insurance data.

🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, KPIs, dynamic visuals, and business logic.

🔗 Data Modeling – Relationships established among insurance tables to enable cross-filtering and interactive reporting.

📈 Interactive Visualizations – KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Bar Charts, Column Charts, Matrix Tables, Line Charts, and Slicers.

📁 File Format – .pbix for dashboard development and .png for dashboard preview.

4. Data Source

Source: Prism Insurance Pvt Ltd Insurance Dataset (Sample Insurance Dataset)

The dataset contains insurance-related information including policy details, customer demographics, policy types, premium amounts, claim amounts, claim status, policy status, and other business-related attributes. The data has been cleaned, transformed, and modeled to create meaningful KPIs and interactive reports that support insurance performance analysis and business decision-making.

5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

Insurance companies manage large volumes of policy, customer, premium, and claims data. When this information is maintained in spreadsheets and disconnected reports, it becomes difficult to monitor business performance and identify important trends.

Key questions such as:

What is the total number of insurance policies?

What is the total premium amount?

What is the total claim amount?

Which policy type has the highest number of policies?

Which policy type generates the highest premium?

How are policies distributed across different customer segments?

What is the distribution of claim status?

Which customer demographics contribute to the insurance business?

How does insurance performance change over time?

...are difficult to answer quickly using raw insurance data.

• Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive Prism Insurance Pvt Ltd Business Analytics Dashboard that:

Enables organizations to monitor insurance business performance.

Helps insurance teams analyze policy and premium trends.

Supports claim monitoring and business performance evaluation.

Provides insights into customer demographics and policy distribution.

Assists business leaders in making informed insurance decisions through interactive visualizations.

Reduces the time required to analyze insurance data and prepare business reports.

• Walkthrough of Key Visuals
Key KPIs (Top Section)

Displays important insurance business metrics including:

👥 Total Policies: Number of insurance policies in the dataset.

💰 Total Premium Amount: Total premium collected or recorded.

📉 Total Claim Amount: Total claim amount recorded.

📊 Policy Performance: Overview of insurance business activity.

🛡️ Policy Distribution: Breakdown of policies across different policy types.

📈 Business Trends: Interactive analysis of insurance performance.

Policy Type Filter Panel

An interactive slicer allows users to filter all dashboard visuals based on selected policy types, helping users explore specific insurance categories and compare their performance.

Premium Analysis by Policy Type

Displays premium performance across different policy types, helping insurance teams identify policy categories contributing to overall premium collection.

Claim Analysis by Policy Type

Compares claim amounts across different policy types, allowing organizations to understand claim distribution and monitor insurance business performance.

Policy Distribution by Customer Demographics

Visualizes insurance policy distribution across customer demographic segments, enabling HR and business teams to understand customer composition and insurance coverage patterns.

Claim Status Analysis

Shows the distribution of claims based on their status, helping insurance teams monitor claim activity and understand the overall claims landscape.

Policy Status Analysis

Provides insights into the distribution of active and inactive policies, supporting policy monitoring and business performance evaluation.

Premium and Claim Trend Analysis

Analyzes insurance performance trends over time, helping organizations identify changes in premium collection and claim activity.

Department-wise / Category-wise Business Analysis

Displays the distribution of insurance business across relevant categories, providing a clear overview of business performance and supporting strategic planning.

• Business Impact & Insights

Insurance Performance Monitoring: Insurance teams can monitor policy and premium performance through interactive KPIs and visualizations.

Claims Analysis: Claim-related insights help organizations understand claim distribution and identify areas requiring further analysis.

Customer Segmentation: Demographic analysis supports a better understanding of customer composition and insurance coverage patterns.

Policy Management: Policy distribution and status analysis help teams monitor business activity and support operational planning.

Strategic Business Planning: Management can explore insurance trends and use data-driven insights to support business decisions.

Data-Driven Decision Making: Interactive Power BI reports make insurance data easier to understand, analyze, and communicate.
